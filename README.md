# One Step

*Working name.* A cooking agent that learns the dishes you already cook and shows you
the ones you're **one step away from** — written as only what's different from your version.

> **Kabsa: your usual rice, but…**
> - mix baharat from spices you have
> - add a dried lime
> - cook the chicken in the rice pot

Most recipe apps match by flavor. One Step connects dishes by **cooking method**, so
chicken with broth rice leads you to kabsa, plov, jollof and Hainanese chicken rice.
